1. Which part(s) of the body does the exercise affect well?
A. Knees
B. Back
C. Hands
D. Legs ✅
OK everybody. This next exercise is a bit difficult, but it’s really good for your legs. All you do is put your back against the wall, place your feet about half a metre away from the wall, move your back down the wall so your knees are bent at 90 degrees. Now put your hands out straight in front of you, make sure your head is against the wall. Now, see how long you can stay like that. If you do two minutes, you’re doing well.
